AI Georeferencing

Any map. Any projection. Seconds, not hours.

Upload a scanned sheet and Amano identifies the coordinate system, finds matching control points, and georeferences it automatically — including local mine grids, UTM zones and custom projections. Export as GeoTIFF or shapefile.

Automatic CRS detection

No manual guessing of the projection. Amano reads the map and proposes the datum and grid, with a confidence score you can check.

  • UTM & local mine grids
  • Custom & historical projections
  • Datum transforms handled

Smart control points

Matches graticule crosses, grid ticks and named features automatically, then lets you refine any point by hand.

  • Sub-pixel registration
  • Residual / RMS reporting
  • Human-in-the-loop edits

Clean outputs

Georeferenced rasters ready for your GIS, with a full audit trail from source scan to database record.

  • GeoTIFF & world files
  • Full provenance log
  • Batch processing
Full Digitization

Polygons, lines and points — extracted from the map.

Beyond georeferencing, Amano digitizes geological units, fault lines, drill-hole markers, contours and any other features into editable vector layers. Refine, attribute, and export as shapefiles.

Geology & structure

Lithological units and fault traces vectorised as polygons and lines, ready to attribute and style.

Points & symbols

Drill collars, sample sites and outcrop markers captured as point layers with their labels.

Edit & export

Review every feature, fix what you need, and send clean vectors into your database. Accuracy improves over time.
